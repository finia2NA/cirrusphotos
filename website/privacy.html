Privacy

  • Sign-in credentials and trusted-device tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain.
  • The app connects only to your own NAS, and to this website to check for updates.
  • There is no analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • Photo metadata and thumbnails are cached on your Mac and stay there.
  • The app keeps diagnostic logs on your Mac. Nothing is sent automatically: to report a bug you can export a log file (Help > Export Logs) and attach it to a GitHub issue yourself.

The source code is public, so you can check these statements at github.com/finia2NA/cirrusphotos.
